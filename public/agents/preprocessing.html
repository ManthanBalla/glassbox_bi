What does this agent do?

This agent cleans raw tabular metrics into an aligned, gap-free, forecast-ready time-series dataset. Every transformation is logged into an explainable audit trail. To preserve model integrity and avoid data leakage, data is never normalized or scaled here (scaling is handled downstream by the Forecasting Agent).
