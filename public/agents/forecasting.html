What does this agent do?

This agent takes a cleaned time-series dataset from the Preprocessing Agent, evaluates candidate models on a strictly chronological holdout, selects the winning model objectively, refits on all data, and generates future forecasts with 80% and 95% prediction intervals. Every step is explainable.
